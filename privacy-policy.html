Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

Home / Privacy Policy

  Last updated: October 2026

1 Purpose of Data Use

At ERBE — Legacy of Time, we collect your personal data solely for the purpose of enhancing your shopping experience. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Notify you about new product arrivals, exclusive collections, and special offers.
  • Remember your preferences and past interactions so we can provide you with a more personalized and seamless experience each time you visit.

We do not use your data for any purpose other than what is described in this Privacy Policy.

2 Data Collection

When you interact with our website, place an order, or contact us through any of our channels, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name — to address you properly and process your orders.
  • Email Address — to send you order confirmations, updates, and promotional content (with your consent).
  • Phone Number — to coordinate deliveries and provide customer support via WhatsApp or call.

We only collect the minimum amount of data necessary to fulfill your orders and improve our services.

3 Data Sharing

Your trust is paramount to us. We want to assure you that:

  • Your personal data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.
  • We do not disclose your information to external companies, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • Your data is used exclusively by ERBE — Legacy of Time for the purposes outlined in this policy.

4 Cookies Policy

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve functionality and enhance your browsing experience. Cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our site and allow us to remember your preferences.

  • You can disable cookies at any time through your web browser's settings.
  • Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features on our website.
  • By continuing to use our website without changing your browser settings, you consent to our use of cookies.

5 Data Security

We take the security of your personal information very seriously. To protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse, we implement the following measures:

  • SSL Encryption (Secure Sockets Layer) — All data transmitted between your browser and our website is encrypted using industry-standard SSL technology, ensuring that your information cannot be intercepted during transmission.
  • Standard Security Protocols — We follow best-practice security protocols and regularly review our systems to prevent data breaches and leaks.
  • Access to your personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6 User Rights

As a valued customer, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Update or Correct — You may request to update or correct your personal information at any time by contacting us.
  • Right to Unsubscribe — You can opt out of our marketing emails at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link included in every promotional email we send.
  • Right to Delete — You may request the complete deletion of your account and all associated personal data by contacting us via email or WhatsApp.

To exercise any of these rights, please reach out to us using the contact details provided below.

7 Policy Updates

ERBE — Legacy of Time reserves the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy at any time as our business practices evolve or as required by applicable law. When changes are made:

  • The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
  • We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your data.
  • Your continued use of our website and services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
